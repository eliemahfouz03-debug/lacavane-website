BEFORE YOU COME

Good to know.

A countryside idea, carefully taking shape. Here is what is planned—and what comes later.

Locate Khenchara village ↗

Village location only. The estate parcel has not been confirmed.

Is La Cavane already open?

No. It is a university business concept with visitor opening planned for January 2028. Dates, hours and final offers are not confirmed.

Is the wine made on site?

Opening tastings and retail use wine from licensed Lebanese producers. Our winery is planned for 2030, with first La Cavane sales in 2031. The early vineyard is for demonstrations and visits.

Can families visit without a tasting?

Yes, the concept includes vineyard outings, food and alcohol-free options. Children’s and non-tasting activity prices need a tailored quote. Tastings follow a proposed 18+ policy.

Can we book lunch or a visit separately?

Yes. The planner lets you select components individually. Table & Vine simply combines a visit-and-tasting estimate with an average meal estimate, without a package discount.

What about private groups?

Use the private-event enquiry. Planned events accommodate around 30–60 guests, subject to approval, and may replace ordinary restaurant service. A proposed 30% deposit is shown only as an estimate.

Can we stay overnight?

No. Accommodation is outside the current project plan.

What happens after I submit?

You see a demonstration summary that you can copy or print. Nothing is sent, no booking is confirmed and no payment is taken.
